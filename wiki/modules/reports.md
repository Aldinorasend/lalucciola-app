# Reports

Owner (full) and Manager (view) only. The metrics below were confirmed by the owner (D-031).

## Booking reports
- Covers per day, week and month (booked vs. actually seated)
- Bookings by source (phone / WhatsApp / walk-in)
- **No-show rate** and cancellation rate
- Busiest days and time slots (heatmap)
- Table utilisation (% of available table-time used)
- Average waitlist wait time

## Inventory reports
- Current stock value (total and per category)
- Usage per item over a period
- **Waste** per item and its cost over a period
- Stock-count variance (adjustments) per period
- Expired stock written off

## UX
- A date-range picker with presets (today, this week, this month, custom).
- shadcn `chart` + `card` (see [ui-components](../architecture/ui-components.md)).
- CSV export for every report (D-030).
